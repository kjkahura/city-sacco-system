# Email Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Email notifications after the reference platform: each SACCO's SMTP settings, templates of type EMAIL with recipients and subscriptions, delivery through the existing dispatcher, manual email, and subscriptions changed by staff and by members in the portal. The channel parts are shared, so SMS adds a provider on top.

**Architecture:**
- **Channels (`src/domain/notifications/channels/`):** `index.js` keeps the settings of each channel in `notification_channels`; `email.js` validates, tests and sends through nodemailer.
- **Templates:** `notification_templates` gains `subject`, `recipient` and `recipient_role`, and the content type `HTML`.
- **Delivery:** the dispatcher resolves recipients when an event is processed, checks subscriptions, queues `EMAIL` messages, and sends them through the channel with a per-type pace.
- **Subscriptions (`notifications/subscriptions.js`):** a row per template and member, read and changed by staff and by the member in the portal.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22, Express 5, Postgres 16, nodemailer 10 (smtp-server for tests).

**Spec:** `platform/docs/audits/audit-email.md`. On 5 October 2026 John accepted all ten defaults, and asked for members to change their own subscriptions in the portal (decision 4).

## Global Constraints

- **Vendor name:** never in code, docs or commits.
- **Writing style:** no em dashes.
- **Settings:** From Name, From Email, Reply-to, SMTP Host, SMTP Port, Transport Encryption (`SSL_TLS` on 465 or `STARTTLS` on 587), Username and Password (sealed, never returned). Each SACCO's own; no platform fallback. The switch starts off.
- **The guard:** the SMTP host resolves to public addresses only (checked at connection time); TLS is required with certificate checks. `SMTP_ALLOWED_PORTS` and `SMTP_EXTRA_CA_FILE` exist for tests and development.
- **Recipients:** `CLIENT` (the holder: the member, or the group itself), `CREDIT_OFFICER` (the loan's officer, else the holder's), `GROUP_ROLE` (the group members holding `recipient_role`).
- **Subscriptions:** apply to members and groups, not credit officers. Opt-out templates send unless the recipient unsubscribed; opt-in templates send only to those subscribed. Manual email ignores subscriptions.
- **Outcomes:** `MISSING_EMAIL_RECIPIENT` (no address, no attempt), `EMAIL_SERVICE_NOT_ENABLED`, `INVALID_SMTP_CREDENTIALS`, `MESSAGING_EXCEPTION`. Temporary failures (4xx, network, timeout) follow the webhook retry schedule; permanent ones (5xx, refused sign-in) fail at once.
- **Pace:** 60 a minute per SACCO by default (`pacePerMinute`).
- **Permissions:** SEND_MANUAL_EMAIL (new; managers and administrators). Settings are read with the template permissions and changed by administrators. Templates use CREATE_ and EDIT_COMMUNICATION_TEMPLATES.

## Review Focus

1. A placeholder value can never add HTML to a body, or a header line to a subject.
2. The SMTP password is never returned, logged or sent in clear.
3. The SMTP host cannot reach a private address, including through DNS.
4. A member can change only their own subscriptions, and only for active email templates.
5. An unsubscribed recipient gets no automatic email; manual email still reaches them.
